# Research and Validation Plan

This document outlines the structure for the critical review of the proposed AI implementation.

## 1. Oversight Committee

-   **Chair:** Director of AI Research
-   **Mandate:** To receive and synthesize the findings from the specialized research committees, make a final judgment on the proposed strategy, and authorize any revisions.

## 2. Specialized Research Committees

### a. Committee for Architectural Soundness & Simplicity (CASS)

-   **Leader:** Dr. Anya Sharma
-   **Mandate:** Scrutinize the proposed architecture for over-engineering, hidden complexity, and long-term maintenance overhead. Evaluate the robustness and elegance of the solution, favoring simplicity and durability over convoluted systems.

### b. Committee for Performance & Scalability (CPS)

-   **Leader:** Dr. Kenji Tanaka
-   **Mandate:** Validate all performance, speed, and scalability claims made in the analysis. Stress-test the proposed architecture against projected future loads and assess its long-term viability and potential bottlenecks.

### c. Committee for Goal Alignment & Efficacy (CGAE)

-   **Leader:** Dr. Elena Petrova
-   **Mandate:** Ensure the proposed solution directly and efficiently solves the core business problem—improving document retrieval relevance. Evaluate whether the chosen path delivers the highest possible return on investment in terms of accuracy and user satisfaction.

## 3. Process

1.  Each committee will conduct a focused review of the documents (`DEEP_ANALYSIS.md`, `HYBRID_IMPLEMENTATION.md`, `FINAL_RECOMMENDATION.md`) based on its specific mandate.
2.  The findings will be synthesized into a single, comprehensive report.
3.  The report will provide a definitive judgment on the current plan and, if necessary, propose a revised, superior implementation strategy.
